STRAIGHT TALK

Owned Media vs. Earned Coverage

We keep this line clear — because credibility is the whole business.

Owned · Ours

The Culture Report

The Culture Report is Digital Diego’s own publication. A review, feature, or interview there is real editorial coverage from our platform — and it’s a genuine asset in an artist’s story. But it is our platform, and we will always say so plainly.

Earned · Independent

Independent Coverage

Coverage from an independent outlet is earned — an outside editor or writer decides the music is worth their audience’s attention. That’s what our outreach works toward. It is decided by them, on their timeline, and it is never ours to promise.

Our Standard

We do not guarantee press coverage, interviews, playlist placements, or viral results — no honest publicist can. What we guarantee is the work: a story built properly, materials that respect an editor’s time, outreach aimed at the right desks, and clear reporting on what landed and what didn’t. Placement is earned. Effort is promised.

QUESTIONS
Before You Ask

    

      

        Can you guarantee coverage or playlist placement?
        
No — and be careful with anyone who says they can. Independent editors and curators make their own decisions. We guarantee the quality of the story, the materials, the targeting, and the follow-through — and we report honestly on results.

      

      

        Is a feature on The Culture Report the same as independent press?
        
No. The Culture Report is Digital Diego’s own publication, so coverage there is owned media — valuable, real, and part of your story, but distinct from earned coverage by an independent outlet. We label it that way because your credibility with media depends on the distinction.

      

      

        When should I start a campaign?
        
Before the release, not after it. Publicity needs lead time — editors plan ahead, and a story pitched on drop day is already late. If your release is set, the consultation should happen now.

      

      

        What do you need from me to start?
        
The music, your story so far, your links, and your timeline. Use the consultation brief above — it covers everything we need to give you a straight read on what a campaign could look like.
