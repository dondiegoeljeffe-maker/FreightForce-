    The short answers, straight.

    What does the $199 cover?

    The build. Your hosted artist website — designed and put together from your tracks, photos, video and bio, live on the Digital Diego network with your music, booking contact and email signup in one place. It's a one-time start cost; the monthly keeps it running.

    What does the $99/month actually do?

    It keeps the machine moving: hosting, site updates when you send new music or dates, release posts prepped from your assets, executive admin support, and a monthly check-in so you always know what's live and what's next.

    What if I need more than that?

    That's the add-on lane — extra content, a full release campaign, a PR push, video cuts, or heavier booking and admin support. Tell us what the next move is, we scope it and quote it in writing. Nothing extra runs — or gets charged — without that quote in front of you.

    Do I need to be tech-savvy or send files every week?

    No. You make one drop at the start — tracks, photos, video, bio, city, links — and after that it's one ask a month from us. The whole point is that the website and admin stop being your second job.

    Is this only for rappers?

    No. It's built for independent artists who release music and need a professional home base plus help running the business side — any genre, as long as you're actively putting music out.
